Application lifecycle management

{%- endif -%} {%- if slot == 'list_item_description_2' -%}

Our software operators enable you to use a single codebase to automate the lifecycle management of your data applications across public clouds, Kubernetes, virtual (VM) and bare metal machines.

This is possible thanks to Juju and charms. Juju is an open source orchestration engine for software operators that enables the deployment, integration, and lifecycle management of applications at any scale, on any infrastructure. A charm is an operator – business logic encapsulated in reusable software packages that automate every aspect of an application's life.
