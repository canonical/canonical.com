Find your
passion

Finding and investing in your passion is core to flourishing at Canonical. We all benefit from effective team players.

We support our team members’ self-driven growth and learning with our Training and Tuition program. It offers all team members an annual training budget of USD 2,000 to enhance their skill sets and develop professionally. We champion independent thinkers who chart their own course to excellence.

We also partner with high-quality content providers, to make sure our people get access to the best possible training materials, and the time to benefit from them.
